My Flutter package · pub.dev · MIT

Pinch to change how much a list says, not how big it is.

Pinch out and every entry grows from a title, to a one-line summary, to the full notes. The font size never changes. Words already on screen slide to their new spots, new words fade in around them, and the entry under your fingers stays exactly where it is. It's Ted Nelson's 1970 idea of StretchText, driven by a pinch.

Roshen: mobile engineer.

Try it: pinch on a trackpad, or Ctrl + scroll, over this text.

Why it's not a toy

  • Fast: 97–99% of frames inside the 8.3 ms budget of a 120 Hz screen, with 1,000 entries, on a mid-range phone
  • Lag-free scroll anchoring via a custom sliver that corrects the offset during layout
  • Accessible: each entry is an adjustable control for VoiceOver and TalkBack; honours reduce-motion
  • Every language: RTL, CJK and Thai per character, everything else per word, including Sinhala
  • LLM-ready: works with rewritten summaries, ships a prompt and a checkVersions validator
  • Android, iOS, web, macOS, Windows and Linux; WASM-ready; perfect 160/160 pub points

02 — How I build

Four habits behind every release.

  1. 01

    Offline-first, always.

    Warehouses, truck cabs, supermarket counters and hospital corridors don't come with perfect Wi‑Fi. My apps keep working without a connection and sync when they can.

  2. 02

    Privacy is a feature.

    Healthcare data at Dedalus; on-device AI in Aqua Tag. The safest user data is the data that never leaves the device.

  3. 03

    Sweat the frame budget.

    semantic_zoom keeps 97–99% of frames inside 8.3 ms on a 120 Hz screen, with 1,000 entries. Smooth isn't a nice-to-have.

  4. 04

    Built for everyone.

    Screen-reader support, reduce-motion and real localisation (English, French, Italian, Sinhala) from day one, not as an afterthought.
