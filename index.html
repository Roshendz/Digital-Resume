02 — How I build

Four habits behind every release.

  1. 01

    Offline-first, always.

    Warehouses, truck cabs and supermarket counters don't come with perfect Wi‑Fi. My apps keep working without a connection and sync when they can.

  2. 02

    Close to the metal.

    Feitian POS SDKs, barcode scanners, printers, USB storage, FTP — I'm at home where software meets hardware.

  3. 03

    Privacy is a feature.

    Aqua Tag edits photos, PDFs and video entirely on-device. The best way to protect user data is to never collect it.

  4. 04

    Shipped beats perfect.

    A track record of delivering to customer spec, on time — and automating the busywork so teams can focus on what matters.
