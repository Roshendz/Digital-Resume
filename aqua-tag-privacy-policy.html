Privacy Policy for Aqua Tag

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Welcome to Aqua Tag. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Aqua Tag collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Your Photos, PDFs, and Videos Stay on Your Device

All photo, PDF, logo, collage, and video editing happens entirely on your device. We never upload, store, or have access to the images, documents, or videos you edit in the app. Saving or sharing your work uses your device's own gallery and share features — nothing is sent to our servers.

Information We Do Collect

To keep the app working well and to improve it, Aqua Tag collects a limited amount of the following information:

Anonymous usage analytics (via Google Firebase Analytics)
We collect anonymous data about which screens and features you use — for example, which tools are opened, whether an export completed, or how many pages a scan had. This never includes the content of your photos, documents, or videos, and no personal information is attached to it.

You can turn this off at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data. It's enabled by default but fully optional.

Crash and diagnostic reports (via Google Firebase Crashlytics)
If the app crashes or encounters an error, we automatically receive a technical crash report (e.g. what part of the app failed) to help us fix bugs. This does not include your photos, documents, or personal information.

Feedback you submit
If you use the "Send Feedback" option in Settings, the name, email address, and message you provide are sent to our secure database (Google Firebase Firestore) so we can read and respond to your feedback. This is entirely optional — you're never required to submit feedback to use the app.

Subscription/purchase information
Aqua Tag uses RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions (currently not active for most users). If you make a purchase, RevenueCat processes anonymized purchase and entitlement data on our behalf to manage your subscription status. We do not have access to your payment card details — payments are handled directly by the Apple App Store or Google Play.

Third-Party Services We Use

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Firestore, Authentication) — see Google's Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) — see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy

These providers may process data on servers outside your country, under their own privacy and security practices linked above.

Your Choices

  • Disable anonymous analytics anytime in Settings → Privacy
  • Feedback submission is entirely optional
  • You can request deletion of any feedback you've submitted by emailing us below

Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for any data sent from the app. Firebase and RevenueCat both enforce encrypted connections.

Children's Privacy

Aqua Tag is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
